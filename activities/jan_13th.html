Jan. 13th [Tue.]

Boeing Everett Factory

10:00 ~ 11:30

1 Tour the Boeing Everett Factory

  • An 80-90 minute guided tour inside the world's largest building as of 2026, offering a firsthand look at the aircraft assembly process.
  • Tour the 777 and 777X assembly lines: Observe the assembly of the next-generation flagship aircraft, the 777X and the 777 Freighter, from a private balcony.
  • Accompanied by a professional guide: A Boeing professional guide will accompany you on your journey, providing detailed explanations of aircraft history, design principles, and the unique operations of the Everett factory, including transportation within the factory.

2 Visit the Boeing Future of Flight Center

  • Aviation Gallery: Presenting current and future aerospace technologies, exhibits include autonomous flight systems, sustainable fuels, space probes, and a cross-section of the 787 Dreamliner fuselage.
  • Sky Deck: A rooftop observation deck offering panoramic views of the Paine Field runways and the Everett factory.

Amazon Skill Center and Headquarters

14:00 ~ 16:00

1 Amazon Skill Center Tour

  • Cloud Discovery Space: An interactive exhibition hall where you can experience, through all five senses, how cloud technology is applied to our daily lives and industries.
  • Experience a variety of industries: Six key exhibits showcasing how the cloud is being used in real-world settings, including robotics, space technology, machine learning, gaming, smart homes, and sports (e.g., NFL data analytics).

2 Visit Amazon Headquarters and Experience Amazon Go

  • The Spheres: The iconic building at Amazon's headquarters in Seattle, USA, and a giant greenhouse in the heart of the city
  • Amazon Go: An innovative unmanned convenience store operated by Amazon. Core technology: "Just Walk Out" (utilizing computer vision, sensor fusion, and deep learning technologies)
