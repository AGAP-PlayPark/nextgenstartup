Jan. 10th [Sat.]

ThinkCity 2026 Hackathon

08:30 ~ 18:00 @U301

1 Theme

Let's collaborate across cultures, solve real community problems, and create a smarter, more connected future for our cities. 3) Host: City of Bellevue, Bellevue College, COSS Project, MLH

2 Host

Bellevue College

3 Participants

191 participants (54 students from the COSS Immersive Media Business Unit, 137 college and high school students from Bellevue and Seattle areas)

4 Event Goals

  • Encourage students from Washington State and abroad to participate in civic problem-solving.
  • Strengthen collaboration between Bellevue College, the City of Bellevue, leading universities, and industry.
  • Strengthen Bellevue's role as a regional hub for smart city innovation.
  • Operate a pilot event that can grow into an annual statewide civic hackathon.

5 Challenge Themes

Smart Mobility & Transportation, Energy Efficiency & Sustainability, Public Safety & Community Well-Being, Digital Equity & Accessibility, Citizen Engagement & Communication Tools, Data-Driven Government Services, Emergency Preparedness & Resilience, Environmental Monitoring (air, water, green spaces), etc.

6 Mentors and Judges

  • Mentor: Daniel Lai (City of Bellevue) and 28 others
  • Judges: Janice Zahn (Washington State Representative) and 14 others

7 Event Schedule

  • 08:30 AM - 09:00 AM: Registration and Networking Breakfast
  • 09:00 AM - 09:30 AM: Opening Ceremony, Project Presentation & Team Formation
  • 09:30 AM - 12:30 PM: Workshop 1 - Ideation and Initial Prototyping
  • 12:30 PM - 01:15 PM: Lunch and Guest Lecture
  • 01:15 PM - 03:15 PM: Workshop 2 - Prototyping and Refinement
  • 03:15 PM - 03:30 PM: Break and Project Summary Submission
  • 04:00 PM - 05:00 PM: Judging and Team Presentations
  • 05:00 PM - 06:00 PM: Dinner, Awards Ceremony, and Closing Ceremony
