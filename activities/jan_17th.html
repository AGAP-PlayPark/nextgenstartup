Core Hypotheses and Field Research Design
Team 01
  • Problem: Private car sharers (Hosts) are severely stressed by unclear damage (especially hygiene issues such as cigarette odor) that occurs after returning their vehicles.
  • Solution: Hosts would prefer a solution that monitors and prevents vehicle damage in real time, rather than simply managing it after the fact.
  • Feature: Among temperature, noise, and smoking detection features, "Smoke Detection" is likely the most sought-after feature by users.
  • Price: Hosts would be willing to pay a monthly subscription fee if it is proven to prevent vehicle damage and reduce cleaning costs.
Team 02
  • Do citizens perceive the existing civil complaint system as a "one-way submission system"?
  • Do they perceive CITY's community-based structure as a differentiated way of engaging?
  • Do the upvotes, comments, and rewards structure positively influence participation and return visit intentions?
  • Do concerns about the platform structure primarily stem from operational, trust, and authentication issues, rather than the concept itself?
Team 03
  • Collection Efficiency: Minimize unnecessary collection visits based on waste load volume.
  • Route Optimization: Shorten collection routes with selective collection using real-time data.
  • Overflow Prevention: Prevent and manage waste overflow through real-time monitoring
Team 04
  • Safety: Measure crosswalk travel times and remaining time by target.
  • Energy: Map streetlight brightness status relative to pedestrian traffic.
  • Maintenance: Collect photos of broken or abandoned streetlights.
  • Interview: Guerrilla interviews with pedestrians and drivers.
Team 05
  • Have you experienced any specific anxiety while using existing navigation apps?
  • How would route selection change if actual incident information were available?
  • Are there any criteria or conditions under which you feel it's acceptable to increase travel time for safety reasons?
Team 06
  • Observe and record external factors that cause delays while using public transportation, and collect user feedback on perceived inconveniences.
  • Factors users consider when selecting a public transportation app.
Team 07
  • Validation: Confirm if 'noise' is a real pain point for residents.
  • Solution Fit: Check if 'Sound Scout' solves their problems.
  • Privacy Check: Understand resistance to public audio data collection.
  • Market Viability: Test 'Willingness to Pay' (WTP) for the service.
Team 08
  • Could you share your experiences where you've faced significant difficulties with traffic during an emergency dispatch?
  • When approaching a red light with your sirens on, do you trust that the signal will 100% change, or do you always approach with caution, ready to brake just in case it doesn't?
  • Seattle often has rainy or foggy conditions. Do you notice any difference in how quickly or reliably the signals transition during such weather?
  • We understand the current Opticom system utilizes IR and GPS for signal preemption. Are there any specific pain points or areas where you feel this system could be improved to better support your operations?
Team 09

  • When making an entry decision (Go/No-Go), what information do you lack the most right now that you decide to 'guess'?
  • Do you think why these robot technologies are not universally employed at the fire scene yet to fire fighting technologies?
  • Would you be willing to use our assistant robot?
  • Do you see any limitations or areas for improvement in our technology?
