T-Mobile

13:00 ~ 16:00

1Experience the T-Mobile Headquarters Testing Lab

Visit the T-Mobile Launchpad (Device and Technology Lab) at T-Mobile's Bellevue headquarters.
  • Antenna Chambers (Anechoic Chambers): Tests how accurately smartphone and IoT device antennas transmit and receive signals by completely blocking external radio waves and eliminating internal reflections.
  • Network Virtualization & Cloud Lab: Tests technology that implements network functions solely through software, without physical equipment.
  • Interoperability Testing Lab (IOT Lab): Checks whether the latest devices from manufacturers like Samsung, Apple, and Google, as well as drones, robots, and industrial sensors equipped with 5G modules, are fully compatible with the network.
  • 5G Innovation Lab: Tests in real-world environments how ultra-high frequencies (mmWave) above 28 GHz penetrate obstacles (walls, glass, etc.) and how far low-band frequencies can reach.
  • Life & Performance Lab: We tested under extreme conditions how much battery drain and how much heat the device generates when using 5G.
