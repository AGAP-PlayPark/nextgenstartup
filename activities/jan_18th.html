Team 04 NE 4th St & Bellevue Way NE intersection (downtown Bellevue) and surrounding areas Senior citizens, strollers, pedestrians with children, etc.
  • The missing 5.2 seconds: The golden time for survival. Risk exposure rate: 50% of surveyed individuals remained on the road after the traffic light ended or arrived just before it ended.
  • Energy efficiency: Wasted light. With the introduction of AI Smart Dimming, energy savings of up to 90% are expected in this area.

Team 08 Seattle Station 10 Firefighter
  • Pain points related to Opticom and signal systems: Opticom receivers are not installed at all traffic lights, so it is not always possible to be sure that Opticom will work.
  • Pain points related to navigation route planning: When setting a route, factors such as on-site events, traffic volume, time of day, road width, and fire truck size must be considered, but the current navigation system does not reflect these factors.
