Workshop - Essential Elements of a Compelling Business Plan

10:00 ~ 12:00 @N201

1 Topic

Essential Elements of a Compelling Business Plan

2 Instructor

Ron Wiener (Venture Mechanics)

3 Key Lecture Content

  • What We Do : Ex) 'A Safer, Easier Way To Pay' (PayPal)
  • Problem : Who is your customer? What problem do they have today that only you can solve?
  • Solution : What is your solution? Why is it unique? Why has nobody done this before? What has changed that allows room for a startup here?
  • Market Size : SOM, SAM, TAM
  • Customers : What kind of customer discovery has been conducted? Are there customers that matter with a high willingness to pay?
  • Per Customer Economics : What is the revenue model? What are customers willing to pay? What is the projected lifetime value of these customers (LTV)?
  • Go to Market Strategy : How will you get in front of the customer? Is there a force-multiplier channel that can allow this company to get their product to customers quickly and profitably?
  • Customer Acquisition Costs : What is the projected customer acquisition cost (CAC)? What will it cost to service them?
  • Competition Overview : How is the company positioned in the landscape with all the other companies I've seen?
  • How are companies in this category valued?
  • Technology Status : If there's a big IP piece, several slides on the state of technology development.
