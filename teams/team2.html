Team 02

Civic Innovation Idea Portal

Project Overview

Description

Develop a web platform where citizens can propose, vote, and collaborate on civic innovation ideas, integrating gamification to encourage participation.

Expected Output

A full-stack web app with authentication, idea submission, upvoting, and analytics on engagement trends.
