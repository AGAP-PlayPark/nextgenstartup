Team 11

AK-ACD (Ambiguous Clearance Detection) ADAS

Project Overview

Description

This project focuses on developing a next-generation Advanced Driver Assistance System (ADAS) that addresses safety challenges faced by drivers of large vehicles in narrow or confined spaces, such as alleyways and tight parking areas. By integrating camera-based vision with mono depth map technology, the system estimates available clearance and compares it with vehicle dimensions and safety margins to provide intuitive, real-time feedback to drivers. The proposed solution aims to reduce minor collisions that can escalate into serious accidents or operational delays, particularly for novice drivers and commercial vehicle operators.

Main Goals and Objectives

The goal of this project is to improve driving safety for large and commercial vehicles in confined spaces by developing an ADAS that accurately estimates available clearance using camera-based depth information and provides intuitive feedback to drivers.

Expected Output

The expected outcome is a prototype clearance-detection ADAS algorithm with validated depth estimation performance, supported by scenario-based demonstrations and technical documentation.
