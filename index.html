NextGen Startup Challenge 2026
Smart City and Civic Innovation



Hosted by Bellevue College Computer Science Department

and Korean University Partners (COSS Immersive Media, COSS Future Mobility, JNU RISE)

Bellevue College, Bellevue, Washington, USA


The NextGen Startup Challenge fosters creativity and teamwork by uniting Korean and U.S. students in an exciting competition focused on Smart City and Civic Innovation. Over two dynamic weeks, participants strengthen global connections while developing forward-looking solutions that enhance urban living and public well-being.
